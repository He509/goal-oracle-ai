📈 ODDS & MARGINS: WHY CRYPTO BOOKS PAY MORE

The "margin" is the slice a bookmaker keeps from every bet: add up the implied probabilities (100 ÷ odds) of all outcomes, and whatever sits above 100% is the house edge. It is the single number that decides whether a winning tipster actually makes money, and most bettors never calculate it. State monopolies and high-street books often run 15–20% on a 1X2 market; a competitive crypto book runs a fraction of that.
